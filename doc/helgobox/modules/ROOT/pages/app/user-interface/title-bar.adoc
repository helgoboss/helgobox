= Title bar

The bar on the top of the app window is the _Title bar_.
It primarily provides general functions, such as window controls, access to settings, and quick access to the REAPER transport.

Some themes are simply combinations of various appearance settings.
Once you’ve selected such a theme, you can further customize it by adjusting the settings.

The theme called "REAPER" is special in that it enables {term_settings_use_host_colors}, which borrows the colors from your current REAPER theme.
