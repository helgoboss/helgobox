==== Track audio output

image::playtime::generated/screenshots/elements/inspector/column/origin/track-audio-output.png[Track audio output, role="related thumb right", float=right]

Records the audio *output* of a track, post-FX. This is great for recording the output of virtual instruments.

This feature uses REAPER loopback audio hardware channels under the hood. That means it only works if you have at least 2 loopback channels enabled. You can enable them
in "REAPER Preferences → Audio → Virtual loopback audio hardware channels".

You should choose a recording track that's different from the playback track. Otherwise, other clips playing on the playback track will be recorded as well. Probably not what you want ;)
