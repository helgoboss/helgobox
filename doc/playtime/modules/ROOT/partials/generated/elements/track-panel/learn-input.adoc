=== Learn track input

image::playtime::generated/screenshots/elements/track-panel/learn-input.png[Learn track input, role="related thumb right", float=right]

1. Press this button.
2. Press a key or move a control on your MIDI controller, play your instrument, or speak into your microphone.
3. Playtime will automatically detect the MIDI or audio source and assign it as the input for this track.
